Is it real,
or is it luck?

TokIO tells you whether a strategy's profit is distinguishable from luck, once you allow for what a Sharpe ratio hides: P&L that isn't independent from bar to bar, and every variant you tried before this one. Every test it runs has a measured false-positive rate.

What every result reports.

check_backtest()

Corrected for every try

A test of the mean that allows for serial dependence, corrected for every variant you tried (Romano-Wolf step-down).

DSR · PSR

Deflated Sharpe

The Deflated and Probabilistic Sharpe Ratios, as published and corrected for dependence, plus the minimum track record.

PBO

Overfitting odds

The probability of backtest overfitting (CSCV) for grids: how often the in-sample winner loses out of sample.

costs=

Costs and breakeven

Costs, turnover, and the transaction cost at which the edge disappears.

lookahead

Catches peeking

The Sharpe with every position taken one bar later. A cheating SPY strategy drops from 13.26 to −0.44.

robustness

Not one lucky stretch

Concentration with both tails trimmed, consistency across time, and the minimum backtest length.
